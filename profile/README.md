# Green Dynamics · 律动造物

**We scale science.**  
让科学无限扩展

Green Dynamics is building closed-loop AI for Science infrastructure that connects scientific models with real experiments. Starting with materials and chemicals, our goal is to help research teams formulate hypotheses, run experiments, and learn from physical-world feedback.

律动造物正在构建面向材料与化工研发的 AI for Science 闭环基础设施，把科学模型连接到真实实验。我们希望帮助研发团队提出假设、开展实验，并从真实世界的反馈中改进后续决策。

## What we build

- **ByteScience** — scientific models and agent systems for materials and chemical R&D.
- **ByteFactory** — AI-native laboratory automation designed to connect models with real experimental workflows.
- **Byte Infra** — the infrastructure connecting model decisions, lab execution, and experimental feedback.

## 我们在构建什么

- **ByteScience** — 面向材料与化工研发的科学模型与智能体系统。
- **ByteFactory** — 连接模型与真实实验流程的 AI 原生自动化实验室。
- **Byte Infra** — 连接模型决策、实验执行和结果反馈的闭环基础设施。
